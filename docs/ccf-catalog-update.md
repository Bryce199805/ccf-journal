# CCF 2026 seventh edition journal catalog

Updated `data/all_journals_correct.json` from the CCF seventh edition, keeping all journal relations in all ten CCF domains. The catalog has 295 domain-level journal rows. Multiple appearances of the same journal remain separate rows under their respective domains.

## Source and verification

- CCF's [official announcement and category directory](https://www.ccf.org.cn/Academic_Evaluation/By_category/) says the seventh edition was published on 2026-03-31 and updated on 2026-04-09 to correct an erratum.
- The official category pages are linked from the CCF directory. Their current static contents do not consistently reflect the formal PDF, so they were not treated as the authoritative row source.
- The formal PDF was inspected as a pinned [PDF mirror](https://github.com/LovenSar/CCF_RANK/blob/55ca5eae67c26d518c6a8b71ee5827a053086f20/第七版中国计算机学会推荐国际学术会议和期刊目录（正式版）-CCF-ABC.pdf), SHA-256 `271b630b576bf8a4f802e767f5694caded93680e22b3a19bef7902591c45c1d3`. Its contents identify it as the 2026 CCF journal and conference catalog. This copy is cross-checked against CCF's announcement; the CCF announcement remains the authoritative publication source.
- The PDF metadata records creation and modification at `2026-04-09 11:13:27 +08:00`, consistent with CCF's notice that the directory was updated on April 9 to correct an erratum. The announcement does not identify the corrected row, so the audit establishes that the whole checked PDF snapshot matches the transcription rather than attributing the erratum to a particular journal.
- The machine-readable transcription is pinned to commit `dcf1a02335fb44dcaf785997d5c535ab41628a16` in [ccf-2026.json](https://github.com/haozhou-wong/ccf-recommended-list-2026-markdown/blob/dcf1a02335fb44dcaf785997d5c535ab41628a16/data/ccf-2026.json). The collection script uses only its journal sections and omits conferences.
- Provenance and collection notes are in [`data/ccf_catalog_meta.json`](../data/ccf_catalog_meta.json).

## Comparison with the previous project data

The previous catalog contained 171 rows across five domains. `data/letpub_full.json` contains 273 records. The updated catalog contains 295 rows across ten domains.

The initial report at [`scraper/output/ccf_catalog_diff.json`](../scraper/output/ccf_catalog_diff.json) is preserved unchanged. Its Python normalizer originally did not equate `&` with `and`, and its 15 removed titles therefore included same-journal name variants. The corrected normalizer now matches [`scraper/lib/identity.js`](../scraper/lib/identity.js). The revised classification is [`scraper/fixtures/catalog/ccf_catalog_diff_revised.json`](../scraper/fixtures/catalog/ccf_catalog_diff_revised.json), with source-backed mappings in [`scraper/ccf_title_aliases.json`](../scraper/ccf_title_aliases.json).

Of the original 15 removed-title rows, six are verified same-identity name aliases, three are title continuations with changed current ISSNs (TASLP, EITEE, and Discover Computing), and six have no target in the current official journal catalog. Three aliases are matched by corrected `&`/`and` normalization; three require explicit aliases. The revised counts are 131 new titles, six titles absent from the current catalog, 133 new domain relations, and nine removed domain relations (six complete removals and three domain-relation removals). The nine reconciled title changes still affect the catalog relation arithmetic, but do not imply nine reusable LetPub identities. The row relation equation is `171 + 133 - 9 = 295`; it counts domain-level catalog relations, not physical journal identity or metrics reuse. TON was not among the 15 removed-title rows and is documented as a separate serial-change finding in the revised diff report. One old B-to-C change plus the World Wide Web Journal C-to-B upgrade make two level changes among the six identity aliases.

The row-by-row PDF audit is [`scraper/fixtures/catalog/ccf_pdf_transcription_audit.json`](../scraper/fixtures/catalog/ccf_pdf_transcription_audit.json): all 295 domain/level/sequence rows matched, with 291 unique journal titles. Four titles have two domain relations each: TOMM, DKE, IPL, and IJIS. The audit also records per-domain A/B/C counts. The transcription had a line-wrap space in `JCOMPLEXI TY`; the catalog stores the PDF abbreviation as `JCOMPLEXITY`. Empty official abbreviation cells remain empty; no LetPub abbreviations are borrowed. IMWUT is genuinely absent in this edition; it was not mapped to a conference or another journal.

## Rebuilding

Run `python3 scraper/collect_ccf_catalog.py` to download the pinned transcription and regenerate the catalog, metadata, and diff. Use `--input-json PATH` to rebuild from a saved copy. The script writes only the three catalog outputs and does not modify import, frontend, backend, or compose files.

### Current serial checks for title successions

Publisher checks have established four CCF title-succession cases with distinct current serials and historical serials. TASLP, EITEE, and Discover Computing are separated from reusable identity aliases in [`scraper/fixtures/catalog/ccf_catalog_diff_revised.json`](../scraper/fixtures/catalog/ccf_catalog_diff_revised.json) under `newSerialContinuations`; TON, which was not among the original 15 removed-title rows, is recorded separately under `additionalIdentityFindings`. All four historical candidates are excluded from matcher aliases in [`scraper/ccf_title_aliases.json`](../scraper/ccf_title_aliases.json), and current identifiers and historical serials are recorded separately in [`scraper/ccf_identity_hints.json`](../scraper/ccf_identity_hints.json).

- IEEE Transactions on Audio, Speech and Language Processing is the 2025–present IEEE title at Xplore publication number 10723155. The current ISSN Portal record is 2998-4173. The prior IEEE/ACM title ended in 2024 with 2329-9290/2329-9304; the older 2006–2013 IEEE title used 1558-7916/1558-7924. Neither old LetPub identity is used as the current CCF identity or as a source of current metrics. IEEE's 2025 title list labels 1558-4173 as eISSN, while the inspected ISSN Portal record does not confirm that medium, so the hint leaves `eissn` null.
- ENGINEERING Information Technology & Electronic Engineering (EITEE) began under its current title in 2026 with print/eISSN 3069-8928/3069-8936. Its publisher states that Frontiers of Information Technology & Electronic Engineering (FITEE), with 2095-9184/2095-9230, was the prior 2015–2025 title. The prior LetPub identity is not used for the current title or its metrics.

The publisher evidence is available from the [IEEE Xplore current journal page](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/xpl/mostRecentIssue.jsp?punumber=10723155), [ISSN Portal current TASLP record](https://portal.issn.org/custom-search/print/2998-4173/public), [Zhejiang University Press EITEE introduction](https://www.fitee.zjujournals.com/en/introduction/), and [EITEE publisher site](https://www.academax.com/EITEE/home).

Springer explicitly says Information Retrieval Journal became Discover Computing in 2024; its former serials 1386-4564/1573-7659 ended in 2023. The current Springer page lists eISSN 2948-2992, not 2948-9679. Historical LetPub ID 3566 is excluded from current identity/metrics reuse. See the [Springer journal page](https://link.springer.com/journal/10791) and [ISSN Portal record](https://portal.issn.org/resource/ISSN-L/2948-2984).

- IEEE Transactions on Networking is the current IEEE title, with Xplore publication number 10723154 and electronic ISSN 2998-4157. ISSN Portal confirms 2998-4157 as the current online serial and cites 2025 Xplore issues. The previous IEEE/ACM title used 1063-6692/1558-2566 through 2024. Its former identity/metrics are not reused for the current title. See [IEEE Xplore](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/xpl/aboutJournal.jsp?punumber=10723154), [ISSN Portal](https://portal.issn.org/resource/ISSN/2998-4157), and [IEEE’s May 2025 title list](https://open.ieee.org/wp-content/uploads/IEEE-Title-List-May-2025.pdf).
